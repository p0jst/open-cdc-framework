Open CDC Framework · Tool 02

Team Skill Matrix

Map your team's skills, spot the gaps, and decide what to train, mentor or hire. Each team member fills in the self-assessment sheet; you upload the returned files here. Re-uploading a sheet for the same person updates their entry. Everything stays in your browser — no data leaves this page.

1
Distribute the sheet.
Send every team member the self-assessment: Excel template · CSV version. It takes ~15 minutes to fill in.
2
Collect & upload.
Drop the returned files below — several at once is fine. New sheets for the same person overwrite their previous answers, so re-run the exercise any time.
3
Read the picture.
The heatmap shows who can do what; the gap view compares against role expectations; the actions view suggests what to train, who can mentor whom, and what to hire for.
Drop filled sheets here or click to choose files (.xlsx or .csv)
You can also load a demo team to see how it works, or import a saved snapshot.

Data lives only in this browser (localStorage). Export a snapshot to archive a review round, move to another machine, or share with a co-manager.
